GoFundMe Campaign: Help Sustain Lisa Hartouni's Work

The campaign is live and raising funds. This page is a hub for everything you need to give, share, or stay updated — directly from this site.

In September 2026, a series of malware attacks took down Lisa Hartouni's websites — years of archived work, client contacts, and the digital presence she'd built over decades. She launched Help Sustain Lisa Hartouni's Work to rebuild, and to do something she hasn't done before: build lasting infrastructure so a single hack can never hold her work hostage again.

About the Campaign

Lisa has spent more than 46 years using photography and community storytelling to document the people, neighborhoods, and movements that shape Southern California. From organizing tenants and building neighborhood associations in Santa Monica, to working as a photojournalist, documentary photographer, and web designer, she has built a life around making change visible.

That work lives in three long-term projects: Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods — a classroom-based project now reaching hundreds of 5th-grade and university students in Long Beach; From Here to Manzanar — documentation of the Japanese American families forcibly removed from Terminal Island to the camps during World War II, nearing completion; and a moon photography series built over years of patient observation. Her photographs have also documented voting rights in southwest Alabama, Los Angeles police abuse, and 1987 immigration detention in Inglewood.

Now Lisa needs help to preserve and sustain that work. Contributions will restore her websites, set up print sales so she can earn from her archive, and lay the groundwork for a more stable path forward.

Where the Money Goes

The campaign has two milestones, and both are real:

$2,000 — First unlock. Initial security response and site isolation. Restore access to existing archives and content. Set up tools to accept commissions and sell prints again. Lay groundwork for grant eligibility, which requires an active digital presence.

$15,000 — Full goal. Rebuild all three hacked websites from scratch. Full CMS restoration and database recovery. Security hardening so this can't happen again. Etsy storefronts and e-commerce setup. Fiscal sponsorship, so future donations can become tax-deductible. A sales and marketing plan to rebuild her client pipeline.

Note: GoFundMe shows a $2,000 microgoal on the campaign page that must be reached before the full $15,000 target unlocks. The $15,000 is the real campaign goal.

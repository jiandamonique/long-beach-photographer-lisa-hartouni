Listed in the Arts Council for Long Beach Artist Directory

I'm pleased to share that I am now listed in the Arts Council for Long Beach artist directory as a Long Beach documentary photographer and photojournalist.

The directory includes three bodies of work that span nearly four decades:

  • INS Detention Centers (1987) — photographs from an immigration detention protest in Inglewood, documenting a motel that had been converted into a facility where children were held.
  • Moon Series (2017) — an ongoing collection of full-moon photographs across all four seasons, developed over years of night observation.
  • Manzanar (2025) — a new project documenting the WWII incarceration of the Japanese American community from Terminal Island — the first community sent to Manzanar — combining photography with interviews and written testimony from people who were interned.

Being part of the Arts Council for Long Beach directory is a step toward making this work more discoverable — by curators, educators, event organizers, and community members looking for artists with roots here. It also opens doors I want to walk through: local exhibitions, community events, commissions, and grant programs that look for artists who are actively embedded in this city's cultural life. Being in the directory is one way to show that I am.

I've been a documentary photographer and photojournalist in Long Beach for over 40 years. The work has covered immigration, civil rights, community portraiture, and the threads of history that run through this region. Getting my online presence rebuilt and reconnected is part of making that archive accessible again — and connected to where it belongs.

If you're interested in my work for an event, exhibition, or commission, you can reach out through the contact page or find me through the Arts Council for Long Beach directory.

The GoFundMe campaign to rebuild my web presence is still open — every donation and share helps.

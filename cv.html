Long-Term Projects

2008–present
Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods
Ongoing documentary series spanning twelve or more classrooms and community settings across Los Angeles County. Recipient of an ArtRelease grant. Exhibited at Hot Java, Long Beach. Project site: intersectionsproject.com
Ongoing
From Here to Manzanar
Documentary project on the history of Japanese American incarceration, focused on Terminal Island and East San Pedro. Combines photography, recorded interviews, and testimony from survivors and descendants. Project site: fromheretomanzanar.com
1987
INS Detention Centers
Documentary series photographing immigration detainees held at the Mardi Gras Hotel in Inglewood, California — one of several informal detention facilities operated by the Immigration and Naturalization Service during that period. Featured in the Press-Telegram, 2014.
Ongoing
Moon Series
Fine art photography series capturing the moon across seasons and conditions. Published on the Intersections Project Facebook page and through Facebook's Moon Lovers photography group.
Ongoing
My Photo Records: Three Decades of Photos
Six photo essays spanning 1980 to the present, drawing from four decades of negatives, slides, and digital files documenting community life, protest, and social change in Southern California.

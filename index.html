About

From community organizing to documentary photography

  • 1980

    Graduates from UCLA with a BA in Anthropology and Sociology.

  • 1981

    Joins the VISTA Volunteer program, training as a community organizer.

  • 1980–1997

    Works with the Pico Neighborhood Association in Santa Monica as staff photographer, and holds a range of community-organizing roles — housing organizer, program developer, fundraiser, administrator — while freelancing as a news photojournalist, published in national and local newspapers and books.

  • 1987–1989

    Manages the Midnight Special Bookstore in Santa Monica, later helping develop it into the Midnight Special Bookstore Cultural Center.

  • 1997–2000

    Returns to school to study new media, web design, and digital technology at the Academy of Entertainment and Technology, Santa Monica College, graduating with a degree in Interactive Media.

  • 2001–present

    Designs and maintains websites; digitizes negatives, slide film, and VHS tape; and continues developing Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods, an ongoing interactive community project.

Frequently asked

Common questions

  • Who is Lisa Hartouni?

    A documentary photographer, photojournalist, and community storyteller based in Long Beach, California, with four decades of work spanning community organizing, photojournalism, and ongoing projects like Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods and From Here to Manzanar.

  • How can I support Lisa's work?

    Through her GoFundMe campaign, directly via Venmo or PayPal (see the Support section above), or by hiring her for portrait, event, restoration, or web design work.

  • What is Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods?

    An ongoing community photography project begun in 2008, asking participants — from 5th graders to university students — to walk their own neighborhoods and photograph what catches their attention. See the Photographs page for more.

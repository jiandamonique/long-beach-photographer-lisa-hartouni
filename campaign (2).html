Who Lisa Is

A Career Built Around Making Communities Visible

⚠ Her websites are currently offline. Three domains — years of archived work, client contacts, and commission pathways — were taken down by a hack in September 2026. The longer they stay down, the harder it becomes to rebuild momentum.

Lisa Hartouni has spent decades using photography and community storytelling to document the people, neighborhoods, and movements that shape Southern California. From organizing tenants and building neighborhood associations in Santa Monica, to working as a photojournalist, independent photographer, and web designer, Lisa has built a life around making change visible.

Over the years, that work has grown into deeply rooted long-term projects: Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods, archival work connected to the Manzanar Pilgrimage, and a moon photography series created through years of patient observation. Her photographs have documented social justice struggles involving detention, voting rights, and police abuse — always grounded in the belief that communities deserve to see themselves reflected with care.

Now Lisa needs help to preserve and sustain that work. Contributions will restore her websites, set up print sales so she can earn from her archive, and lay the groundwork for a more stable path forward — one where a hack can never hold her work hostage again.

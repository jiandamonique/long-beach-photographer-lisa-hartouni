## Projects

**Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods** · *Ongoing since 2008, Long Beach*
Participants walk their own neighborhoods and photograph what catches their attention. Used in more than a dozen classrooms, supported by an ArtRelease grant, exhibited at Hot Java.

**From Here to Manzanar** · *In progress, 2017–present*
Documents the WWII incarceration of the Japanese American community from Terminal Island — the first community sent to Manzanar. Combines photography with interviews and written testimony.

**My Photo Records** · *1980–present*
A portfolio of black-and-white documentary and newsprint photography drawn from six photo essays: voting rights in Alabama, LA police abuse, INS detention centers, housing insecurity, and more.

**Moon Series** · *Personal, ongoing*
A personal project photographing the full moon across every season, built through years of patient night observation.

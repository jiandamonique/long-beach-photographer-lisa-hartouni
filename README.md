# Lisa Hartouni

Four decades of pointing a camera at the truth.

Lisa Hartouni is a documentary photographer, photojournalist, and community storyteller based in Long Beach, California. Her work has followed neighborhoods, movements, and moments of change since the early 1980s — from voting rights in Alabama to police accountability in Los Angeles, to her ongoing "Intersections: Portraits of Neighborhoods," built directly out of Long Beach classrooms and community walks.

This site is a temporary home for Lisa's story, her photographs, and the press that's covered her, while her permanent website is rebuilt. A team of volunteers put it together and keeps it running.

- **About** — `index.html` — her story, timeline, and how to reach her
- **Photographs** — `photographs.html` — the long-term projects
- **Press** — `press.html` — everything written about her, newest to oldest
- **Shop** — `shop.html` — prints and originals
- **Blog** — `blog.html` — news and campaign updates

If her work means something to you, [click here](index.html) to visit, and every page links to ways to help.
